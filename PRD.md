# SmartCampus AI — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview
SmartCampus AI is an AI-powered campus complaint management platform for universities and colleges. It enables students to submit complaints and uses AI to classify, prioritize, summarize, detect duplicates, and recommend the correct department. Staff manage and resolve complaints, while administrators monitor campus-wide performance through analytics.

**Tagline:** Smarter Complaints. Faster Resolution. Better Campus.

## 2. Goals
- Centralize campus complaints in one system.
- Reduce manual complaint routing.
- Improve complaint visibility and accountability.
- Detect urgent issues early.
- Give students transparent complaint tracking.
- Provide administrators with actionable analytics.
- Create a realistic, professional SaaS-quality campus workflow.

## 3. User Roles
### Student
Submit complaints, upload evidence, track status, receive notifications, communicate with staff, and provide feedback.

### Staff / Department Officer
Review assigned complaints, inspect AI analysis, assign/reassign, communicate, update status, add resolution notes, and close complaints.

### Administrator
Monitor all complaints, manage departments/users, monitor escalations, view analytics, generate reports, and configure system settings.

## 5. Complaint Categories
Academic, Hostel, Maintenance, IT/Internet, Transport, Library, Security, Cafeteria, Electrical, Plumbing, Cleanliness, Administration, and Other.

## 6. Complaint Statuses
Pending → Under Review → Assigned → In Progress → Resolution Submitted → Resolved → Closed.

Critical complaints may be escalated at any stage.

## 7. AI Workflow
Student submits description → AI analyzes text → category and urgency are predicted → department is recommended → confidence is displayed → user/staff confirms → complaint is routed → AI can summarize and detect similar complaints.

AI suggestions must be reviewable and overridable by authorized users.

## 10. Non-Goals for Initial MVP
- Full ERP replacement.
- Real payment processing.
- Biometric authentication.
- Production-grade AI training pipeline.
- Integration with every existing college system.

## 11. Demo Data
Use fictional students, departments, complaint IDs, and statistics. Clearly identify demo analytics as fictional.

## 12. Acceptance Criteria
The prototype is successful when a student can submit a complaint, receive an AI analysis, track its lifecycle, staff can process it, an administrator can monitor it, and the student can submit feedback.
